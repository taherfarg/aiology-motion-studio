- Episode ID / title / intended audience:
- Learning objective: what can the viewer explain afterward?
- One-sentence takeaway:
- Technical source(s), checked date, and claims requiring verification:
- Common misconception and how the visuals correct it:
- Recording file, measured duration, and speaking style:
- Narrative: question/hook → concrete example → mechanism → useful takeaway:
- Visual metaphor, its limits, and literal labels:
- Color mapping maintained across scenes:
- Typography and isolated English terms:
- Scene density and reading holds:
- Sound plan: voice priority; licensed music/SFX only when useful:
- Required assets / licenses:
- Review criteria and creator preferences:

Separate factual corrections, readability or timing defects, and optional taste choices. Do not create a new recording or synthetic voice to fill missing narration.
