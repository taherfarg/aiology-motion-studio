# Premium studio kit and Episode 001 v6

Goal: AIOLOGY videos should look and sound like a professional studio production while keeping the white identity, Egyptian Arabic typography and the creator's recorded voice. Chosen direction: premium white-studio 2.5D (approved by the creator). Scope: reusable kit plus Episode 001 v6 as the showcase, with license-safe sound design.

## Look (approved)

- One hero object per shot fills 55-65% of the usable width. The whole canvas is composed; the brand bug appears only at the start and end.
- Three depth layers per shot move at different speeds under a slow camera push with a slight perspective tilt (CSS 3D). The canvas stays pure white; depth comes from layered contact shadows and a soft floor shadow, not gradients, dark fills or glow.
- Matte "clay" surfaces in the existing palette with a top light, an edge highlight and layered shadows. `BRAND.md` documents the materials.
- Kinetic Arabic type reveals whole words (never letters), with emphasis underlines. Captions are a soft pill with the same phrase timing.
- Real overlapping transitions (`@remotion/transitions`), with a matched-object handoff that keeps the shared object opaque. Optional motion blur only on fast moves.

## Sound (assumed defaults, creator may override)

- Sound effects and the music bed are generated by `scripts/make-sound.ts` from fixed-seed synthesis, so they are original work with no third-party license. No voice, no TTS, no paid services.
- Mix: music bed -26 dB below full scale peak at most, effects quieter than the future voice. Levels are measured with FFmpeg; the creator must listen before release.
- Cues are declared per episode in a sound plan and rendered through `@remotion/media` `Audio`.

## Pipeline

- Kit lives in `src/kit/` and is exported from one index. Episode 001 v6 lives in `src/episodes/episode001/premium/` as composition `Episode001Premium`, using the existing `silent-preview.json` timing and captions (script text unchanged). Earlier compositions and exports are preserved.
- Export: `render:episode-001-premium` writes `aiology-episode-001-premium-v6.mp4` (H.264, CRF 16, AAC), checked by `export:check` in its normal (audio) mode.
- Tests: kit timing helper, sound plan bounds and generated asset presence, plus the existing suite. CI continues to run `npm run check`.

## Non-goals

True 3D / Three.js, generated narration, publishing, changes to the episode manifest (still draft until the creator's recording arrives).

## Honest limits

Timing remains provisional until the recording exists. Visual quality is verified by decoded frames and playback metadata, and aesthetic acceptance belongs to the creator.
